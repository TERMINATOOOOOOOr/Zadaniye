{{ site.name }} watches footage from a fixed road camera and finds traffic events in it: accidents and near misses, red-light running, wrong-way driving, prohibited U-turns and turns, vehicles stopped on the carriageway, jaywalking pedestrians, congestion, obstacles and smoke. Every event comes back as a time segment with one of 14 classes, and in parallel each frame gets a probability that an accident starts within the next 5 seconds. The object detector is learned; the event rules themselves are transparent and tunable.
